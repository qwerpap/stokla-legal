Stokla · Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

The short version

Stokla keeps your stock book — items, barcodes, places, suppliers and every movement — only on your iPhone. We have no server that receives it, no account and no sign-in. We cannot see your inventory.

What stays on your phone

Item names, barcodes, units, costs, alert levels, places, supplier names and contacts you type, and the history of receipts, shipments, recounts and moves. It is stored in the app's private storage, protected by iOS file protection. Deleting the app deletes it.

Backup and CSV files are created only when you tap “Save a backup file” or an export, and go only where you send them (Files, iCloud Drive, Mail, Messages…). Those services handle the file under their own terms.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes while the counter screen is open. Frames are analysed on the device in real time; no photo or video is recorded, stored or sent.

Notifications

If you allow them, low-stock alerts are created on the device when an item reaches its alert level. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Counter Pass is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see card details. To check which plan is active we use Adapty, which receives an anonymous app-generated ID, device model, OS version, country, App Store transaction data and simple events such as “paywall shown”. Adapty does not receive your stock book. We do not use advertising IDs and do not track you across apps.

Children

Stokla is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete any item, entry or the whole app at any time. For questions about the anonymous purchase record write to netkaktakto@gmail.com and include the ID shown in the email the app prepares.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
