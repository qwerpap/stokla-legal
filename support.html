Common questions

The camera won't read a code. Try the torch button, hold the phone 10–20 cm away, or type the number under the bars and tap Find.

“The last digit doesn't match.” EAN and UPC codes carry a check digit; a damaged label or a typo makes it fail. Re-type it from the label.

Moving to a new phone. Backroom › “Save a backup file”, put it in iCloud Drive or send it to yourself, then on the new phone Backroom › “Restore from a backup”.

Restoring Counter Pass. Backroom › “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Cancelling. Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Stokla.
