Using Stokla

Stokla is a stock-keeping tool. The counts it shows are the sum of the entries you make. It is not accounting, tax or legal software, and you remain responsible for checking counts against your physical stock and your records.

Free use and Counter Pass

Without a subscription you can keep up to 30 items, receive and ship stock, set alert levels, search, export a stock list and make backups. Counter Pass unlocks unlimited items, places and moves, recounts, low-stock notifications, suppliers with ready-to-send orders, and a CSV of every movement.

Counter Pass is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly). If a free trial is offered, it is shown on the purchase screen; at the end of the trial the price shown is charged unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited when you buy a subscription. Introductory prices, where offered, apply to the first period only.

Your data

Your stock book is stored on your device and is yours. Keep backups: we cannot recover data from a lost, reset or replaced phone. See the Privacy Policy.
